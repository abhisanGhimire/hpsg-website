The Problem

Scammers pick on the people we love

Scammers know many older adults are polite and trusting. They use fake calls, texts, and emails to rush people into sending money. Here are three they love:

SCAM!📞 Fake grandchild emergency“Grandma, I’m in trouble and need money fast. Please don’t tell Mom!”
SCAM!🏛️ Fake IRS call“You owe back taxes. Pay today or you will be arrested.”
SCAM!🎁 Gift-card request“Buy four gift cards and read me the numbers on the back.”
📊 Demo statistic: In our made-up survey of 1,000 imaginary grandparents, 7 out of 10 got a suspicious call last month.
(Concept project. Source: invented for the demo. A real version would cite a real source.)

How It Works

One scam call, four steps

  1. The call

    Grandma gets a call saying her grandson needs money right away.

  2. Detection

    HPSG spots scam warning signs like “urgent” and “gift card”.

  3. Warning

    It warns her on screen and texts a family member.

  4. Safe!

    The family confirms it’s a scam and no money is lost. 🎉

🕵️ Try the Scam Checker

Paste a message and Shieldy will look for common scam warning signs.

Products

Meet the HPSG family of gadgets

HPSG Router Hub

The main unit. Connects to your home Wi-Fi and has a small screen that shows if everything is safe.

  • Wi-Fi 6 speeds
  • 3.5-inch touch screen
  • Works with up to 40 devices

Smart Cameras

Check your doors and rooms from your phone. Everyone in the family gets alerts.

  • 1080p picture
  • Night vision
  • Wire-free in 2 minutes

Smoke & Gas Detectors

The light glows green when you’re safe. In danger it turns red with a loud alarm.

Status: Safe (silent demo)

Safety Alert System

A deterrent: a very loud siren plus flashing strobe lights that scare off intruders and alert everyone in the house.

  • 120-decibel siren
  • Strobe lights

Portable HPSG

A pocket device with GPS so family can always find each other.

$25

Server Bank

24 TB of storage for large homes and businesses, to keep camera video safe.

$3,200

🚑 In an emergency: HPSG alerts the owner first. If the owner confirms, or doesn’t respond, it contacts emergency services.

    Questions people ask

    Do I have to pay every month?

    No! You pay once for your package. There is one optional yearly update.

    What if I have no internet for a day?

    The detectors and siren still work on their own battery (about 10 hours in our pretend spec).

    Who sees my camera videos?

    Only you and the family members you choose. Videos stay on your Server Bank.
